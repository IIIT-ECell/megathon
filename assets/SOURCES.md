- `images/logos/ecell.png`: normal E-Cell logo from https://ecell.iiit.ac.in/assets/imgs/ecell-logo.png.
- `images/logos/megathon-white.png`: Megathon X mark, `public/megathon_logo.png` in https://github.com/IIIT-ECell/megathon-2026.
- `images/hands.webp`: hero artwork, `public/hands.webp` in https://github.com/IIIT-ECell/megathon-2026.
- `images/aftermovie.jpg`: Megathon 2025 aftermovie thumbnail, https://img.youtube.com/vi/RD6PUHd8KUI/maxresdefault.jpg.
- `fonts/MonumentExtended-*.otf`: Monument Extended by Pangram Pangram, free personal-use weights from https://pangrampangram.com/products/monument-extended. Personal licence only; replace with the commercial licence before launch.
- `images/sponsors/lloyds-technology-centre.svg`: `Digital RGB/LTC_Logo_Horizontal_Black_RGB.svg` from the official `LTC 2025_Logos` brand package supplied by the sponsor. The black horizontal lockup, because the sponsor tiles are white; the package's reverse (white) and stacked lockups are in the same archive if a dark tile is ever needed. Replaces the earlier `images/sponsors/lloyds.svg`, which was a Lloyds Bank placeholder for the wrong entity.
- `images/sponsors/vanguard.svg`: Vanguard wordmark from https://corporate.vanguard.com/etc.clientlibs/corporatesite/clientlibs/clientlib-site/resources/icons/vanguardLogos/VanguardLogo.svg.
- `images/sponsors/bhashini.png`: Bhashini logo extracted from the co-branded logo SVG served by the official site, https://bhashini.gov.in/. Trimmed to the artwork; raster, because the site ships no standalone vector of the horizontal lockup. Swap in official vector artwork if the sponsor supplies it.
- `images/sponsors/qualcomm.png`: existing sponsor logo from https://megathon.in/img/sponsors/Qualcomm.png.
