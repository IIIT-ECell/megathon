About Megathon

Since 2016, E-Cell IIIT Hyderabad has brought students together at Megathon to turn ideas into working solutions. Join a community of builders, designers and aspiring entrepreneurs for a weekend of collaboration and innovation.

Megathon X takes place on 10–11 October 2026 at IIIT Hyderabad, with Lloyds as title sponsor and Qualcomm as challenge sponsor.
